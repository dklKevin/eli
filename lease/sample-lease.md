# Sample lease

This is a sample lease.
It is not a real contract.
Do not sign it, and do not rely on it as legal advice or as an agreement with anyone.
Quill Harbor LLC, Alex Kim, and 18 Quill Street are fictional.
The building is not a real New Haven property.

## 1. Parties

The landlord is Quill Harbor LLC.
The landlord's mailing address is PO Box 4188, New Haven, CT 06511.
The tenant is Alex Kim.

## 2. Premises

The landlord rents to the tenant Apartment 4 at 18 Quill Street, New Haven, Connecticut (the apartment).
The building contains four apartments.
The building was built in 1924.
The tenant uses the apartment only as a private residence.

## 3. Term

The term is one year.
It begins on September 1, 2026, and it ends on August 31, 2027.
The tenant receives the apartment at the start of the term and returns the keys when the term ends.

## 4. Rent

The rent is 1800 dollars a month.
The tenant pays the rent in advance on the first day of each month, starting September 1, 2026.
The tenant mails or otherwise delivers each payment to the landlord at the mailing address in section 1.

## 5. Appliances

The landlord supplies these appliances with the apartment:

- a stove
- a refrigerator
- heating equipment

The tenant keeps the appliances that came with the apartment in repair.
That duty covers the stove, the refrigerator, and the heating equipment for the whole term.
The tenant pays the cost of parts and labor for that repair.

## 6. Other care of the apartment

The tenant keeps the apartment clean and in the condition it was in at the start of the term, ordinary wear excepted.
The landlord keeps the building's roof, exterior walls, common stairs, and common halls in repair.
The tenant does not alter the apartment without the landlord's written consent.
The tenant does not disturb the people in the other apartments.

## 7. Utilities

The tenant pays the bills for electricity and for any fuel used by the stove or the heating equipment.
The landlord pays for water and sewer service to the building.

## 8. Entry

The landlord may enter the apartment at a reasonable time after giving the tenant at least 24 hours' notice, and at any time in an emergency.

## 9. End of the term

When the term ends, the tenant removes the tenant's belongings, leaves the apartment clean, leaves the stove, the refrigerator, and the heating equipment in good repair, and returns all keys to the landlord.

## 10. Sample only

This document is a writing sample for demonstration.
It creates no tenancy and no other legal duty.
